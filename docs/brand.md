# Brand — Check My Next Home

Decided by Aldrin Pinto and finalised on 12 Nov 2026. This file is the site's single source for its identity tokens; the build applies the palette and logo at step M7.1 of the Phase 1 plan.

## Name

**Check My Next Home** — the brand name and the website name are identical, everywhere, with no alternates.

1. Canonical host: checkmynexthome.co.uk (the .com domain 301-redirects to it).
2. Strapline: Your family's next home — check what the listing doesn't tell you.

## Colours — Theme 2, Forest green + brass

1. **Forest green** — `#2F5D3E` — the primary colour.
2. **Brass** — `#C29A3B` — the accent colour.
3. **Cream** — `#FDF8E8` — the light background colour.

Teal is dropped entirely and must never be used. Rightmove owns teal (`#00DEB6`) and Zoopla owns purple (`#8046F1`); this palette does not overlap Rightmove, Zoopla or OnTheMarket.

## Logo

**The One-Line Roof-Tick** — the original Logo 1 (variant 1A, the polished original), chosen on 12 Nov 2026.

1. Ruled out by the user and not to be used: variant 1B (green tick) and variant 1C (bold tick).
2. The asset pack is prepared alongside the brand documents: an SVG master, PNG and JPG exports, a favicon set (16, 28, 32 and 48 pixels) plus a 180 pixel touch icon, a 1024 pixel social avatar on forest green, a reversed version and lock-ups.
3. When the pack is delivered, the site header and favicon use these assets, and the Organization structured data (lib/seo.ts) gains the logo URL.
